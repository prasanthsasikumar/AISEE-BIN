# NUS Computing Tour: image sources

All artwork was supplied by NUS School of Computing on 13 Nov 2026 ("SoCCat and NUS Images").
`make_assets.py` builds every app image (iOS and Android) from the files in `source/`:
it only trims, resizes and places them. The NUS name, crest and SoCCat are NUS's; use them as their guides say.

## Logo (NUSLogo, `nus_logo`)
- `source/nus-computing-logo.png`: the "NUS | Computing" lockup, full colour on transparent
  (`SoC-Computing_COL.png` from the 2023 SoC-Computing pack). Shown whole, on white or the light canvas.
- Colours in it: NUS blue `#003D7C`, NUS orange `#EF7C00` (the app's `NUS.blue` and `NUS.orange`).

## SoCCat (SoCat, SoCatWave, app icon)
- `source/soccat-mascot.png`: "NUS SOCCAT Mascot only.png", the arms-up pose. Home screen and the app icon
  (the whole mascot on NUS blue).
- `source/soccat-waving-page5.png`: the "Waving" pose, cut from page 5 of the SoCCat Mascot Usage Guideline
  (PDF rendered at 400 dpi). The white page around it is made transparent. Arrival and tour-complete cards.
- Mascot rules from the guideline: the name is "SoCCat"; do not change the body ratio, eyes, mouth or pink
  cheeks; no costumes, paws or fingers; no lying-down or looking-back poses; never shown in a negative manner.
  Listed poses: waving, jumping, thinking, laughing, sleeping, studying, playing soccer.
