AISEE-BIN: Flower Dome progress

Status update for the team · 1 October 2026 · Gardens by the Bay, Flower Dome POC
In short: on 1 October we tested phone navigation on site at the Flower Dome.
  • Inside the dome it worked. On a route our tester scanned with the iPhone app, the phone guided her to Points 1, 2 and 3. She heard "Point X ahead" just before each one.
  • Around the entrance it struggled. At Points A, B and C the phone had trouble working out where it was.
  • An Immersal scan fixed the entrance. Using last week's Immersal scan of the entrance, the phone found itself immediately and knew roughly where it was.
  • Joining several Immersal scans into one big map isn't reliable yet. That is the main problem left to solve.

What we learned

  • Phone scans (ARKit) work well where there is texture and steady light. Inside the dome that was enough to guide someone. At the entrance the phone often failed to recognise the place: the surfaces repeat, the barriers carry graphics, and the light changes. A map also recognises the place best near where its scan began.
  • Immersal recognises the entrance much better. But an Immersal map is capped at about 500 photos, so the whole route needs several scans. Lining those scans up is the hard part:
    • Lining them up by hand was out by tens of metres.
    • Automatic matching is unreliable on plants.
    • Today's linking walk gave a better fit, but it doesn't yet agree with the marked points.
  • Two phone scans can't be combined. Each starts from its own origin, and there is no way yet to add to an existing map.
  • Light matters. Tours will run in the daytime, so scans should be made in daytime light too.
  • Remote testing works. With Field Test logs and the editor, we could diagnose problems from overseas while our tester was on site.

Next steps

  • On site (Yvonne): make one Immersal scan of the whole route within the 500-photo limit, split by area (100+ photos at the entrance), ideally in daytime light. If it holds together, we get the whole tour in one map with no lining up needed.
  • App: "Add to an existing map". The phone first finds itself in the current map, then extends it, so weak areas like the entrance can be scanned again in more detail.
  • App: combined positioning. Use Immersal to get a position fix, then let the phone's own tracking carry it between fixes.
  • Accuracy: fix a known iPhone issue in how the phone-side Immersal result is read, then use the linking walk again to settle the five-scan line-up. After that, work on making positioning faster and more accurate.
  • Glasses: not tested at the Flower Dome yet. Test with the glasses on site once an Immersal map covers the route.
